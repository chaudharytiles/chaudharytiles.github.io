Zaroori Jaankari

Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawal

Kya aap material dete hain ya sirf labor?

Hum main focus **Expert Labor Services** par karte hain, lekin material selection mein hum poori help karte hain taaki aapko best quality ke stones aur chemicals (jaise Laticrete) milein.

Italian Marble ki polishing mein kitna time lagta hai?

Ek standard **7-stage Mirror Polish** mein area ke hisab se 5 se 7 din ka samay lagta hai. Hum jaldbazi nahi karte kyunki achi chamak ke liye har stage zaroori hai.

Kya polishing se poore ghar mein dhool udti hai?

Bilkul nahi! Hum **Wet-Polishing technology** use karte hain jisme pani ka istemal hota hai. Isse dhool nahi udti aur aapka furniture aur deewarein ekdum saaf rehti hain.

Kya aap Gurugram ke bahar bhi kaam karte hain?

Filhal humari premium services sirf **Gurugram** (DLF, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road aur aas-paas) ke liye hain taaki hum har site par personal supervision de sakein aur quality maintain rahe.

Tiles ke joints kaale kyu pad jaate hain? Kya solution hai?

Normal white cement jaldi ganda ho jata hai. Hum recommend karte hain **Epoxy Grouting**. Ye waterproof hoti hai, kabhi rang nahi badalti aur saalon-saal tiles ke joints ko naya jaisa rakhti hai.

Chaudhary Expert Tips

Apne ghar ko behtar banane ke liye kuch zaroori baatein

📦 Lot Number Check

Tiles kharidte waqt hamesha box par 'Lot Number' check karein. Alag lot mein shade ka farak ho sakta hai.

💧 Epoxy Grouting

Bathrooms aur Kitchen mein hamesha Epoxy Grout use karein. Ye waterproof hota hai aur seepage se bachata hai.

🛡️ Anti-Skid Protection

Bathroom aur Balcony mein hamesha 'Matte Finish' tiles hi lagwayein taaki fisalne ka dar na rahe.

✨ Italian Marble Care

Marble par kabhi Acid ya Harpic use na karein. Sirf pH-neutral cleaner se hi safai karein.

📐 Extra Stock

Hamesha 5-10% extra tiles mangwayein. Future mein agar koi tile toot jaye, toh waisa hi shade milna mushkil hota hai.

🧱 Leveling Spacer

Badi tiles (2x4 ya upar) lagwaate waqt 'Levelling Spacers' ka use zaroor karein taaki tiles ke beech thokar na bane.

🧼 Kitchen Dado

Kitchen slab ke upar hamesha 'High Gloss' tiles lagayein, taaki tel aur masale ke daag asani se saaf ho sakein.

☀️ Outdoor Tiles

Terrace ya parking mein hamesha 'Full Body Vitrified' tiles hi lagayein, ye dhoop aur heavy load jhel sakti hain.
